About Mara

Fourteen years spent chasing weather, light, and the moments between poses.

Mara Voss works primarily in natural light, on assignments that move between remote landscapes and quiet portraiture. Her frames favor patience over spectacle — waiting for the moment a scene settles into itself.

Prints are archived on cotton rag paper and available in limited editions. Commissions are taken on a rolling basis, with travel worked into every brief.
